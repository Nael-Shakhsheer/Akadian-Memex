Meet your research assistant

Ember writes its research briefs with Google’s Gemini AI. To use it you need your own Gemini API key: a private password that lets this app talk to Gemini for you.

  • Takes about a minute.
  • Google’s free tier writes the brief from the AI’s own knowledge. For briefs backed by web sources, Google requires billing to be turned on for the key.
  • Your key is stored only on this device.

Your ideas are saved instantly even before you finish setup. They’ll be researched as soon as a key is added.

Get your key from Google

Open Google AI Studio ↗
  1. Sign in with your Google account and accept the terms if asked.
  2. Tap Create API key.
  3. Copy the key that appears (use the copy icon next to it).

Treat the key like a password: don’t share it or post it anywhere public.

When you’ve copied it, come back here and tap Next.

App lock

    
    
    

    
Use 12+ characters. Forgotten passphrases cannot be recovered. Keys, ideas and recordings are encrypted at rest, but remain accessible to app code while unlocked. This is a passphrase lock, not Face ID. Switching away locks the view; returning requires unlocking.

    
Sync between devices

    
    
    

    
Opt-in encrypted sync through a secret GitHub Gist. Use the same sync passphrase and Gist ID on both devices. Keys and voice recordings are excluded. Newer edits win; deletions sync too. Sync one device at a time. Secret Gists are link-accessible, so encryption is essential.

    
Instant capture

    
    In iOS Shortcuts, add “Open URLs” with this URL. Assign the shortcut to your Action Button. Ember attempts recording after unlock; if blocked, tap the microphone.
